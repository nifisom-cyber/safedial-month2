
# SafeDial — Week 7 Campaign Results Interpretation

## 1. Campaign Overview

Campaign Name: SafeDial — Help Is One Dial Away

Campaign Duration: 30 Days (Proposed)

Campaign Goal:
To increase awareness of SafeDial and encourage people
to access emergency contact information and safety tips.

## 2. Sample Campaign Results

| KPI | Target | Sample Result | Target Achieved |
|-----|--------|---------------|-----------------|
| Website Visits | 300 | 245 | 81.7% |
| Emergency Button Clicks | 60 | 52 | 86.7% |
| Safety Tips Views | 40 | 38 | 95% |
| Demo Enquiries | 20 | 16 | 80% |
| Engagement Rate | 50% | 48% | 96% |

IMPORTANT:
These are fictional demonstration results.
They do not represent actual SafeDial website performance.

## 3. Results Interpretation

### Website Visits

SafeDial received 245 sample visits against a target of 300.

This represents 81.7% of the target.

Interpretation:
The sample campaign attracted some interest but did not
reach its website traffic target.

### Emergency Button Clicks

The sample results show 52 clicks against a target of 60.

This represents 86.7% of the target.

Interpretation:
Visitors showed interest in emergency contact information.
However, the campaign did not reach its click target.

### Safety Tips Views

The sample results show 38 views against a target of 40.

This represents 95% of the target.

Interpretation:
Safety Tips performed closest to its target among the
four activity-count KPIs.

### Demo Enquiries

The sample results show 16 enquiries against a target of 20.

This represents 80% of the target.

Interpretation:
The demonstration enquiry feature received fewer
submissions than planned.

### Engagement Rate

The fictional engagement rate was 48% against a 50% target.

This is 2 percentage points below the target.

Interpretation:
The sample results suggest that visitor engagement
could be improved.

## 4. Campaign Challenges

- Website traffic was below the proposed target.
- Some visitors may not have noticed the enquiry form.
- Emergency contact buttons may need more visibility.
- The campaign may need stronger promotion.
- No live analytics data has been collected yet.

## 5. Optimisation Recommendations

### Recommendation 1: Improve Social Media Promotion

Share useful safety awareness posts on Facebook,
Instagram and WhatsApp to encourage more website visits.

### Recommendation 2: Make Emergency Buttons More Visible

Place important emergency contact buttons in
clear and easy-to-find positions.

### Recommendation 3: Promote Safety Tips

Share short safety tips with links directing
people to the SafeDial Safety Tips page.

### Recommendation 4: Improve the Enquiry Form

Make the demonstration enquiry form easy to find
and explain its purpose clearly.

### Recommendation 5: Introduce Website Analytics

Connect Google Analytics 4 in the future to
measure real website visits, page views and engagement.

## 6. Conclusion

The fictional sample results show that SafeDial
made progress towards its proposed campaign targets,
but none of the five targets was fully achieved.

Safety Tips views performed closest to the target
among the four activity-count KPIs.

Future improvements should focus on increasing
website awareness, improving navigation and
encouraging visitors to explore safety information.

## 7. Data Limitations

All campaign results in this document are fictional
sample data created for educational purposes.

They must not be presented as actual campaign results.

The existing Local Storage feature only saves
demonstration enquiries in an individual browser.
It does not collect website-wide campaign data.
