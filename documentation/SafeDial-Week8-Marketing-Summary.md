
# SafeDial — Week 8 Marketing Campaign Summary

## 1. Campaign Overview

Campaign Name: SafeDial — Help Is One Dial Away

Campaign Duration: 30 Days (Proposed)

Campaign Goal:
To create awareness about SafeDial and encourage
people in South Africa to access emergency
contact numbers and useful safety information.

## 2. Target Audience

The SafeDial campaign focuses on:
- Young adults and students
- Families and parents
- Community members
- People looking for emergency information

## 3. Marketing Strategy

The proposed campaign will use social media
platforms such as Facebook and Instagram.

The content will include:
- Safety awareness posts
- Emergency information posts
- Educational safety tips
- Website promotional posts

The aim is to encourage people to visit
the SafeDial website and explore its features.

## 4. Sample Campaign Results

| KPI | Target | Sample Result | Target Achieved |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Website Visits | 300 | 245 | 81.7% |
| Emergency Button Clicks | 60 | 52 | 86.7% |
| Safety Tips Views | 40 | 38 | 95% |
| Demo Enquiries | 20 | 16 | 80% |
| Engagement Rate | 50% | 48% | 96% |

IMPORTANT:
These are fictional sample results created
for educational purposes.

They do not represent actual website traffic
or real marketing campaign performance.

## 5. Results Analysis

The sample results show that SafeDial made
progress towards its proposed targets.

Engagement Rate performed closest to its
target, achieving 96%.

Safety Tips Views achieved 95% of its target,
showing that educational content could
encourage interest in safety information.

Demo Enquiries achieved 80%, which was the
lowest percentage of the five KPIs.

None of the five targets was fully achieved.

## 6. Recommendations

To improve future marketing performance,
SafeDial should:

1. Post safety information regularly on
   Facebook and Instagram.

2. Use simple messages and attractive
   promotional designs.

3. Encourage visitors to explore the
   Safety Tips and Emergency Services pages.

4. Include clear calls to action that
   encourage people to visit SafeDial.

5. Introduce website analytics to measure
   real visits and user engagement.

## 7. Conclusion

The SafeDial marketing campaign is designed
to increase awareness of emergency information
and promote safety in South Africa.

The fictional results demonstrate how
marketing performance can be measured
against targets.

In the future, SafeDial should collect
real website analytics to evaluate
its actual marketing performance.

## 8. Project Limitations

The marketing campaign is proposed.

The KPI results are fictional examples,
not actual campaign outcomes.

The current Analytics Dashboard uses
sample data.

The website's browser Local Storage feature
does not collect website-wide analytics.
