## 4. Email Marketing Strategy

### Campaign Name
SafeDial – Stay Informed, Stay Prepared, Stay Safe

### Campaign Objectives

- Increase awareness of SafeDial.
- Encourage people to visit our website.
- Share useful safety and emergency preparedness tips.
- Build a mailing list of interested subscribers.
- Encourage people to prepare for emergencies.

### Target Audience

SafeDial targets four main groups:

1. Students – Need campus safety and emergency information.
2. Parents and Families – Need home safety and family emergency plans.
3. Employees – Need workplace safety information.
4. Community Members – Need community safety awareness.

### Audience Segmentation

Audience segmentation means dividing our subscribers into groups based on their needs.

We plan to send different safety information to each group.

- Students: Campus safety tips.
- Parents and Families: Home and family safety tips.
- Employees: Workplace emergency preparedness.
- Community Members: Community safety awareness.

### Mailing List Strategy

We plan to grow our mailing list through:

- The SafeDial website.
- Social media awareness posts.
- Community safety campaigns.
- Encouraging interested visitors to subscribe.

Subscribers must agree to receive emails and be able to unsubscribe.

Our current React subscription form is a demonstration. It does not save email addresses or send real emails.

### Promotional Email Using AIDA

We used the AIDA marketing model to create our promotional email.

**Attention:** Ask readers what they would do during an emergency.

**Interest:** Introduce SafeDial and explain its features.

**Desire:** Show how SafeDial can help people prepare for emergencies.

**Action:** Encourage readers to visit the SafeDial website.

### Welcome Email

We created a welcome email to thank new subscribers, introduce SafeDial, and explain the safety information they could receive.

### Promotional Email

We created an AIDA promotional email to increase awareness and encourage people to explore SafeDial.

## 6. Email Campaign Performance Measurement

We will measure the success of our campaign using the following metrics:

### Open Rate
The percentage of delivered emails that subscribers open.

Formula:
Open Rate = (Emails Opened / Emails Delivered) × 100

### Click-Through Rate (CTR)
The percentage of delivered emails that receive a link click.

Formula:
CTR = (Recipients Who Clicked / Emails Delivered) × 100

### Conversion Rate
The percentage of website visitors who complete a chosen goal.

Formula:
Conversion Rate = (Completed Goals / Website Visitors) × 100

### Unsubscribe Rate
The percentage of delivered emails that result in an unsubscribe.

Formula:
Unsubscribe Rate = (Unsubscribes / Emails Delivered) × 100

### Example Campaign Results

The following numbers are examples only, not real SafeDial campaign results.

- Emails delivered: 100
- Emails opened: 40
- Recipients who clicked: 20
- Website visitors: 100
- Completed goals: 10
- Unsubscribes: 2

Calculated results:

- Open Rate: 40%
- Click-Through Rate: 20%
- Conversion Rate: 10%
- Unsubscribe Rate: 2%

### Data-Driven Recommendations

1. If the open rate is low, test different subject lines.
2. If the click-through rate is low, improve the call to action.
3. If the conversion rate is low, improve the website experience.
4. If the unsubscribe rate is high, review email frequency and relevance.
5. Compare results across our four target audience groups.
