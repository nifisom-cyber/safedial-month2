
# SafeDial — Week 7 KPI Framework

## 1. Campaign Overview

Campaign Name: SafeDial — Help Is One Dial Away

Campaign Duration: 30 Days (Proposed)

Campaign Purpose:
To raise awareness of SafeDial and help people in South Africa find emergency contact information and basic safety tips more easily.

## 2. Marketing Campaign Objectives

### Objective 1: Increase Website Awareness
Attract at least 300 website visits during the 30-day campaign.

### Objective 2: Encourage User Engagement
Achieve at least 60 clicks on emergency service contact buttons during the campaign.

### Objective 3: Encourage Safety Education
Achieve at least 40 visits to the Safety Tips page during the campaign.

## 3. Target Audience

- Students and young adults
- Parents and families
- Employees
- South African community members

## 4. Important Note

These are proposed campaign targets for a student project.

Actual campaign results have not yet been measured.
Any sample analytics data used later will be clearly labelled as demonstration data.

## 5. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | What It Measures | 30-Day Target |
|-----|------------------|---------------|
| Website Visits | Total visits to SafeDial | 300 visits |
| Emergency Button Clicks | Clicks on emergency call buttons | 60 clicks |
| Safety Tips Page Views | Number of times Safety Tips is viewed | 40 views |
| Enquiry Submissions | Number of demo safety enquiries submitted | 20 enquiries |
| Engagement Rate | Percentage of engaged website sessions | 50% |

## 6. KPI Measurement Methods

### Website Visits
Use Google Analytics 4 to measure website traffic.

### Emergency Button Clicks
Track clicks on emergency service buttons using custom events.

### Safety Tips Page Views
Use Google Analytics 4 page-view reports.

### Enquiry Submissions
Count demonstration enquiries saved in browser Local Storage.
This is only a local test count, not a website-wide total.

### Engagement Rate
Use Google Analytics 4 engagement reports.

## 7. Success Criteria

The campaign will be considered successful if SafeDial
reaches most of its proposed targets within 30 days.

The results will help identify which marketing activities
are working and which areas need improvement.
