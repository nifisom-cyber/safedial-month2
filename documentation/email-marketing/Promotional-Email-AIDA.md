
# SafeDial – Promotional Email Using AIDA

**Campaign:** SafeDial – Stay Informed, Stay Prepared, Stay Safe

**Target Audience:** Students, Parents and Families, Employees, and Community Members

**Subject Line:** 🚨 In an Emergency, Every Second Counts – Discover SafeDial!

**Preview Text:** Don't wait for an emergency to find out where to get help.

---

Hello,

## A – Attention 🚨

**What would you do if an emergency happened right now?**

Emergencies can happen when we least expect them. Knowing where to find help can make a big difference.

## I – Interest 💚

**Introducing SafeDial – Help Is One Dial Away!**

SafeDial is a website designed to make emergency contact information easier to find.

With SafeDial, you can:

- Find important emergency contact numbers.
- Access information about emergency services.
- Learn about emergency preparedness.
- Find useful safety information in one place.

## D – Desire 🛡️

**Be prepared before an emergency happens!**

Whether you're a student, parent, employee, or community member, SafeDial can help you prepare for unexpected situations.

Imagine knowing where to find important emergency contact information when you need it.

SafeDial aims to make this information easier to access.

## A – Action 📲

**Don't wait until an emergency happens!**

Visit SafeDial today and explore the emergency information available.

**Explore SafeDial Now:**

https://nifisom-cyber.github.io/SafeDial/

**SafeDial – Help Is One Dial Away!**

Stay informed. Stay prepared. Stay safe.
