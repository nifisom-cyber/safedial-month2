
# SafeDial – Email Marketing Campaign Plan

## 1. Campaign Overview

Campaign Name: Stay Informed, Stay Prepared, Stay Safe

Brand: SafeDial

Campaign Duration: 14 Days

Campaign Status: Planned (Not Yet Launched)

## 2. Campaign Objectives

- Increase awareness of SafeDial.
- Encourage visits to the SafeDial website.
- Share useful safety information.
- Build an email subscriber list with consent.
- Encourage emergency preparedness.

## 3. Target Audiences

Our campaign targets four groups:

1. Students – Campus safety information.
2. Parents and Families – Family emergency preparedness.
3. Employees – Workplace safety information.
4. Community Members – Community safety awareness.

## 4. Mailing List Strategy

We plan to grow our mailing list through:

- The SafeDial website.
- Social media campaigns.
- Community awareness activities.
- Voluntary email subscriptions.

Subscribers will choose their audience group and agree to receive email updates.

A real mailing list will require secure storage, a privacy notice, and an unsubscribe option.

## 5. Campaign Schedule

| Day | Email | Purpose |
|-----|-------|---------|
| Day 1 | Welcome Email | Introduce SafeDial |
| Day 3 | Safety Tips Email | Share relevant safety advice |
| Day 7 | AIDA Promotional Email | Encourage website visits |
| Day 14 | Safety Awareness Email | Encourage emergency preparedness |

## 6. Email Campaign Measurement

We plan to measure:

- Open Rate
- Click-Through Rate (CTR)
- Conversion Rate
- Unsubscribe Rate

## 7. Example Campaign Results

These are hypothetical results for learning purposes.

- Emails Delivered: 100
- Emails Opened: 40
- Recipients Who Clicked: 20
- Website Visitors: 100
- Completed Goals: 10
- Unsubscribes: 2

Calculated Results:

- Open Rate: 40%
- Click-Through Rate: 20%
- Conversion Rate: 10%
- Unsubscribe Rate: 2%

## 8. Recommendations

- Improve subject lines if open rates are low.
- Improve calls to action if click-through rates are low.
- Improve the website if conversion rates are low.
- Review email frequency if unsubscribe rates are high.
- Compare performance across audience groups.

## 9. Conclusion

SafeDial's email marketing campaign aims to raise safety awareness and encourage people to prepare for emergencies.

The campaign is currently a plan for our CAPACITI Week 5 assignment.

Our website's subscription form is a demonstration and does not currently collect subscriber data or send emails.
