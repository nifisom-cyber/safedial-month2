
# SafeDial — Week 7 Campaign Measurement Plan

## 1. Campaign Overview

Campaign Name: SafeDial — Help Is One Dial Away

Campaign Duration: 30 Days (Proposed)

Campaign Goal:
To increase awareness of SafeDial and encourage South Africans
to access emergency contact information and safety tips.

## 2. Target Audience

- Students and young adults
- Parents and families
- Employees
- Community members in South Africa

## 3. Campaign Channels

### Facebook
Share safety awareness posts and links to SafeDial.

### Instagram
Share short safety tips, educational graphics and stories.

### WhatsApp
Share the SafeDial website link with community groups
where sharing is permitted.

## 4. Performance Measurement

| KPI | Target | Measurement Method |
|-----|--------|--------------------|
| Website Visits | 300 | Google Analytics 4 |
| Emergency Button Clicks | 60 | Custom click events |
| Safety Tips Page Views | 40 | Google Analytics 4 |
| Demo Enquiries | 20 | Local Storage test data |
| Engagement Rate | 50% | Google Analytics 4 |

## 5. Measurement Schedule

### Week 1
Launch the proposed campaign and check whether tracking works.

### Week 2
Review website visits and identify the best-performing channels.

### Week 3
Review engagement and emergency button interactions.

### Week 4
Compare campaign results with targets and prepare recommendations.

## 6. Tools

- Google Analytics 4: Proposed website traffic and engagement tracking
- Browser Local Storage: Existing demonstration enquiry storage
- Google Sheets or Excel: Campaign reporting and calculations

## 7. Data Limitations

Google Analytics 4 has not yet been connected to SafeDial.

Local Storage only saves demonstration enquiries in the
visitor's own browser. It does not provide a central count
of all website enquiries.

Campaign performance figures used in the upcoming dashboard
will be clearly labelled as fictional sample data.

## 8. Campaign Improvement Plan

If website visits are low:
Share more useful safety content and improve campaign messages.

If emergency button clicks are low:
Make emergency contact buttons easier to find.

If Safety Tips views are low:
Add clearer links to the Safety Tips page.

If engagement is low:
Improve website content, mobile usability and navigation.
